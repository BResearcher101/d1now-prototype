About the D1 Now Definitive Trial

We’re testing a youth-friendly way of delivering T1D support in real clinics. The aim: make appointments more useful, boost self-management confidence, and improve outcomes that matter to young adults.

📍

12 centres across Ireland

Recruiting across the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland.

👥

29 participants per site (18–25)

Young adults with T1D aged 18–25 at each centre.

🗓

12-month study

Data at baseline, 6 months, and 12 months.

Core intervention

Intervention Components

🤝

Support Worker

  • Assigned to intervention centres to assist young adults.
  • Focus on goal-setting, engagement and navigating care.
  • Bridges between clinic visits for continuity.
📝

Agenda-Setting Tool

  • Lets young adults set priorities for each clinic visit.
  • Ensures their concerns are heard and addressed.
  • Makes appointments feel more useful and person-centred.
