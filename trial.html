What the trial is

We’re testing a youth-friendly way of delivering Type 1 Diabetes support in real clinics. The aim is to make appointments more useful, boost confidence with self-management, and improve outcomes that matter to young adults.

⏱

Appointments can feel rushed and not tailored to real life.

Our approach

We co-designed the trial with the YAP to make information simple, steps clear, and support more human. Clinics get practical tools; young people get clarity and choice.
