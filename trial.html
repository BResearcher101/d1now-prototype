Participants at a glance

What to expect, in plain language.

⏱

Duration

12 months (baseline, 6 months, 12 months).

📍

Where

Participating young adult diabetes clinics across Ireland.

👤

Who

Young adults aged 18–25 living with Type 1 Diabetes.

🧩

What you’ll use

Agenda-setting tool + support worker (in some sites).

Core intervention

Intervention Components

🤝

Support Worker

  • Assigned to intervention centres to assist young adults.
  • Focus on goal-setting, engagement, and navigating care.
  • Helps keep support connected between clinic visits.
📝

Agenda-setting tool

  • Helps you decide what you want to talk about at your visit.
  • Makes it easier to raise what matters most to you.
  • Aims to make appointments feel more useful and person-centred.
