About the D1 Now Definitive Trial

Overview

We’re testing a youth-friendly way of delivering Type 1 Diabetes support in real clinics. The aim is to make appointments more useful, boost self-management confidence, and improve outcomes that matter to young adults.

📍

12 centres across Ireland

Recruiting across the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland.

👥

29 participants per site (18–25)

Young adults with Type 1 Diabetes aged 18–25 at each centre.

🗓

12-month study

Data at baseline, 6 months, and 12 months.

What it’s like to take part

Taking part is designed to fit around your usual clinic visits. You won’t be asked to do anything unexpected, and your clinic team will guide you through each step.

You can ask questions anytime — and you’re always free to stop if you change your mind.

Core intervention

Intervention Components

🤝

Support Worker

  • Assigned to intervention centres to assist young adults.
  • Focus on goal-setting, engagement and navigating care.
  • Bridges between clinic visits for continuity.
📝

Agenda-Setting Tool

  • Lets young adults set priorities for each clinic visit.
  • Ensures their concerns are heard and addressed.
  • Makes appointments feel more useful and person-centred.
