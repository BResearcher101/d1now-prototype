About the D1 Now Definitive Trial

Overview

D1 Now is a research trial testing a new clinic-based support approach for young adults living with Type 1 Diabetes. The approach was designed with young adults, clinicians and researchers, and is being tested in participating diabetes clinics across Ireland.
The study focuses on a stage of care that can be difficult for many young adults. Moving into adult services often means new teams, shorter appointments, more independence, and more responsibility for day-to-day diabetes management. D1 Now looks at whether care can be made more young adult need focused, connected and responsive during this time.

📍

12 centres across Ireland

The trial is taking place in young adult diabetes clinics across the Republic of Ireland.

👥

29 participants per site (18–25)

We are aiming to recruit young adults aged 18 to 25 living with Type 1 Diabetes.

🗓

12-month study

Participants are followed for 12 months, with study information collected at baseline and 12 months.

Intervention Components

🤝

Support Worker

  • Assigned to intervention centres to support young adults.
  • help you think about what you want to discuss at clinic.
  • support goal-setting and follow-up.
  • help communication between you and your diabetes team.
  • provide a more consistent point of contact around clinic visits.
📝

Agenda-Setting Tool

  • identify what you want to talk about at your appointment.
  • bring up topics that might otherwise be missed.
  • make clinic conversations more focused and useful.
  • support shared decision-making with your diabetes team.

How it works

D1 Now is designed to fit with your usual diabetes clinic visits. It runs alongside your usual care. Over about one year, the study will usually run across three regular clinic visits.
If your clinic is in the intervention group, you may also receive the D1 Now intervention as part of your clinic care. Some participants may be invited to take part in an optional interview afterwards. Your clinic team will confirm what this means at your site.

You can ask questions anytime. Taking part is your choice, and you’re always free to stop if you change your mind.
