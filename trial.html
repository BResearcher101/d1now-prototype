Overview of the D1 Now Definitive Trial

We’re testing a youth-friendly way of delivering T1D support in real clinics. The aim: make appointments more useful, boost self-management confidence, and improve outcomes that matter to young adults.

Study Scope

Number of Centres

  • We are recruiting 12 centres across the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland.
Study Scope

Target Participants

  • Each centre will enrol 29 young adults aged 18–25 with T1D.
Study Scope

Study Duration

  • 12 months with data collection at baseline, 6 months, and 12 months.

Intervention Components

🤝

Support Worker

Assigned to intervention centres to assist young adults with goal-setting, engagement, and managing their diabetes care.

📝

Agenda-Setting Tool

Empowers young adults to set the agenda for their clinic visits, so their concerns and priorities are addressed.
