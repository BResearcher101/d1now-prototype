Young Adult Panel contributions

How the YAP has shaped D1 Now

Since 2014, the Young Adult Panel has helped shape D1 Now through research design, intervention development, trial materials, dissemination, publications, public engagement, grants, workshops, and wider advocacy. This page brings those contributions together in one place.

Overview Research design Publications Presentations Media Grants Awards Workshops

At a glance

Long-term partnership

The YAP is not a one-off consultation group. Members have contributed across multiple phases of D1 Now, from early intervention development to pilot work, trial planning, dissemination and implementation learning.

Since 2014

Long-standing involvement

YAP members have been involved in D1 Now for over a decade, helping keep the work grounded in young adult lived experience.

Research influence

From materials to decisions

Members have shaped topic guides, participant letters, consent forms, information sheets, questionnaires and interpretation of findings.

Dissemination

Sharing the work widely

YAP members have contributed to publications, conferences, invited talks, workshops, media features and public engagement.

Research and study design contributions - Highlights

Co-design in practice
  • Helped develop qualitative topic guides used in D1 Now research.
  • Reviewed and shaped participant invitation letters, consent forms and information sheets.
  • Contributed to questionnaire design and interpretation of qualitative and quantitative findings.
  • Designed a Study Within A Trial on whether a branded gift and letter from a PPI group could improve questionnaire response rates.
  • Contributed to trial recruitment, participant-facing materials, website design, and communication approaches.
  • Supported study leadership and oversight, including representation on study steering structures.
