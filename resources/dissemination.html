Research, publications & dissemination

D1 Now publications and outputs

This page brings together journal articles, protocols, conference outputs, oral presentations, awards, and wider dissemination linked to D1 Now. The work spans young adult type 1 diabetes care, patient and public involvement, intervention development, health psychology, implementation, and trial methods.
