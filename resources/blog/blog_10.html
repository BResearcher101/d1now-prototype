Sean, Eimear and Síofra speak at HRB Ones2Watch Conference

By D1 Now Team • 19 October 2021

The D1 Now team and Young Adult Panel were recently invited to speak at the HRB Ones2Watch conference.

Prof Sean Dinneen and Dr Eimear Morrissey were joined by Young Adult Panel member Síofra Kelleher to present on “Reimagining young adult diabetes care through high-quality public and patient involvement.”

This presentation highlighted how young adults have shaped the D1 Now programme and helped guide the direction of diabetes care research.

You can watch the presentation back on YouTube. The D1 Now presentation starts at approximately 31 minutes.
