I didn’t expect to be listened to

By YAP Member • 2026

When I first joined the Young Adult Panel, I honestly didn’t know what to expect.

I thought it might be one of those situations where you give your opinion, but nothing really changes.

“But that’s not what happened.”

Over time, I realised that what we were saying was actually shaping things — from how materials looked to how the study worked in real life.

That made a huge difference. It felt like being part of something, not just observing it.
