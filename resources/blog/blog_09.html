The D1 Now Study: Origins, Current Work, and Hackathon Experience

By D1 Now Team • 27 January 2021

Prof Sean Dinneen and Dr Eimear Morrissey recently spoke to the team at Diabetes Action Canada about the D1 Now study.

Sean talked about the 2016 Strength in Numbers Conference and hackathon.

The webinar explored how D1 Now developed from early co-design activities into an ongoing research and intervention programme.

Eimear then went through the development of the D1 Now intervention and how the Young Adult Panel acted as co-researchers throughout the process.
