Outputs & contributions

The Young Adult Panel (YAP) is a group of young adults living with Type 1 Diabetes who work alongside the D1 Now team. It is not a one-off focus group or survey. It is a long-term partnership where members help shape research questions, materials, decisions, and how the work is shared.

Guided by experience, driven by passion
🧩

Long-term collaboration

YAP members help keep the work grounded in lived experience — not assumptions. That includes language, design, priorities, and what feels realistic in clinic life.

What makes it different
🌱

Sustainable Mentorship Model

Our panel has been active since 2014, and experienced members often step up as mentors. They support new members so you can contribute in a way that feels comfortable and meaningful.

Meet the YAP

Real people are at the centre of this work. The YAP includes both members and mentors — with mentors being an important part of how this more sustainable PPI model has grown over time.

Why join the YAP?

Joining the YAP means being part of work that can make diabetes research and care feel more relevant, respectful, and useful for young adults.

✨

Make a real difference

Your input can shape real changes in research, resources, and how support is delivered.

🗣

Be heard

Your experience helps keep young adults’ voices at the centre of what happens next.

🧠

Build skills

Gain experience in communication, co-design, research, and advocacy.

What’s involved?

You do not need to do everything. There are different ways to take part, depending on what suits your time and interests.

🧪

Shape research

Help decide what questions matter and what should be prioritised.

📝

Improve materials

Make information sheets, forms, letters, and resources easier to understand and use.

🤝

Work with others

Collaborate with young adults, clinicians, and researchers in a supportive way.

The YAP journey

YAP members have been involved in every aspect of the D1 Now study since the group formed in 2014. Over time, they have helped shape not only this study, but wider thinking around sustainable and meaningful PPI.

2014

The YAP was formed and began meeting regularly, with communication continuing between sessions through online groups, email, messaging, and shared documents.

Research design

Members helped develop topic guides, participant letters, consent forms, information sheets, questionnaires, and interpretation of both qualitative and quantitative findings.

Pilot & SWAT

During the 2020 pilot trial, YAP members designed a Study Within A Trial investigating whether a branded gift and letter from a PPI group could improve questionnaire response rates.

Dissemination & leadership

Members have contributed to conferences, abstracts, competitions, publications, invited talks, and wider dissemination. Two YAP members also sit on the study Steering Committee.

A standout contribution

Strength in Numbers, 2016

One major example of YAP leadership was the international Strength in Numbers conference in NUI Galway in 2016, organised and run by the YAP. Over three days, attendees agreed a core outcome set for clinical trials of interventions for young adults with T1D, three key parts of the D1 Now intervention, and a health technology solution.

Why this matters beyond D1 Now

A more sustainable PPI model

The YAP has helped shift traditional health research culture toward one that includes the patient voice in a more meaningful way. Having been in place since 2014, it is one of Ireland’s longest-standing and most recognised PPI panels, living out the principle of “Nothing about us, without us.”
