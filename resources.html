Resources & Impact

Tools, outputs, and learning designed to be reused.

This hub brings together D1 Now resources, stories, and outputs that can support participants, clinics, researchers, and future studies. Many of these materials were developed with reusability in mind, so they can help shape future PPI work, trials, and youth engagement projects.

Built with young adults through the Young Adult Panel (YAP), and designed to keep growing over time.
