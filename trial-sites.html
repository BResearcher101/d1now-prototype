Overview of the trial

Scope
🏥

12 centres

Across the Republic of Ireland.

👥

348 participants

Young adults aged 18 to 25 living with Type 1 Diabetes and attending participating clinics.

🗓

About one year

The study usually runs alongside regular clinic visits, with key research data collected at the first and final study visits.

The D1 Now Definitive Trial is a cluster randomised controlled trial evaluating the effectiveness and cost-effectiveness of the D1 Now intervention compared with standard care. The primary objective is to assess whether D1 Now reduces HbA1c levels among young adults living with Type 1 Diabetes.
The trial also examines clinical outcomes, patient-reported psychosocial outcomes, implementation and delivery, trial processes, participant experiences, and cost-effectiveness.

How it works

At site level

This section gives site teams a practical overview of the trial flow at clinic level. The exact workflow for each centre should follow the approved protocol, site initiation training, delegation log, and local site arrangements.

Site activation

Before recruitment begins, each site completes the required approvals, agreements, training and delegation steps. The study team will provide site materials, training and guidance before activation.

Responsibilities & support

Working together

The D1 Now trial has been designed to work within busy young adult diabetes clinics. Site teams bring the local clinical knowledge and patient relationships that make delivery possible. The D1 Now study team provides the training, materials, systems and ongoing support needed to keep trial processes clear and manageable.

What sites teams do

  • Complete the necssary training and support setting up the study.
  • Help identify young adults who may be eligible through the approved local screening process.
  • Share approved study information and support the consent pathway.
  • Complete first and final study visit steps using the agreed site workflow.
  • Support trial procedures during routine young adult diabetes clinic care.
  • Enter required study information into the approved systems and documentation.

What the study team supports

  • Provide site initiation training, refresher training and support for new staff joining mid-trial.
  • Provide approved templates, checklists, participant materials and quick-reference guides.
  • Provide named contacts for trial coordination, data, intervention and governance queries.
  • Support REDCap processes, documentation and study workflows.
  • Provide intervention training and delivery support for sites allocated to the D1 Now approach.
  • Offer ongoing troubleshooting so issues can be resolved before they become bigger problems.
  • Centralised lab processes (e.g., HbA1c testing) where applicable.

Ethics, governance & legal requirements

Safety, trust and accountability
Ethics approval Informed consent Data protection Study oversight

D1 Now is delivered within a structured ethics, governance and data protection framework. These arrangements are in place to protect young adults taking part in the trial, support site teams, and ensure that study activity is carried out consistently across participating centres. In practice, this means::

  • Consent is voluntary - young adults receive approved study information, can ask questions, and can decide whether or not to take part.
  • Site roles are clearly delegated - staff carrying out study tasks must be appropriately trained and listed on the Site Training and Delegation Log.
  • Data are handled through approved systems - identifiable consent information and pseudonymised research data are managed separately using approved study processes.
  • Participant confidentiality is protected - research data are recorded using participant study IDs, with local site arrangements for any information that can identify a participant.
  • Oversight is built in - the trial includes sponsor oversight, ethics approval, site governance arrangements and independent study oversight.
  • Templates, SOPs and training support consistency - site teams receive guidance to help keep study delivery clear, documented and inspection-ready.

Site FAQ

Quick answers
What support is provided to sites? +

Training, materials, and a named study contact. The goal is to reduce burden and keep delivery consistent across centres.

Can centres already using an agenda-setting tool participate? +

Potentially — it depends on how the tool aligns with the trial model and what’s needed for consistent delivery. Contact the study team to discuss.

Will previous involvement in the pilot affect recruitment? +

It may shape readiness, but participation depends on the trial design and recruitment plan. The study team can advise.
