D1 Now • Youth-Centred Diabetes Research

Transforming Type 1 Diabetes Care with Young Adults

D1 Now is a research programme co-created with young adults in Ireland, shaping a new approach to diabetes care that values lived experience and shared decision-making.

Learn more ↓
Our Mission

Co-designed research that improves real-world Type 1 diabetes care

We partner with young adults (18–25), clinicians, and researchers across Ireland to build and test practical ways to make diabetes care more supportive, consistent, and youth-friendly.

🤝

Young adults at the centre

Our Young Adult Panel (YAP) co-designs everything—from language to layouts—so the programme reflects lived experience.

🧭

Better clinic conversations

Tools like the Support Worker and Agenda-Setting help prepare for appointments and focus on what matters most.

🔬

12-site national trial

We rigorously test what works across Ireland and share the results openly for services, policy, and future research.
