Transforming Type 1 Diabetes Care with Young Adults

D1 Now is a research programme co-created with young adults in Ireland. We're shaping a new approach to diabetes care that values lived experience and shared decision-making.

What is D1 Now?

D1 Now supports young adults (18–25) living with Type 1 Diabetes. Through a co-designed national trial across 12 clinical sites in Ireland, we’re testing tools and approaches that improve real-world care.

Why it Matters

Young adulthood is a time of change—and diabetes care doesn’t always keep up. D1 Now brings youth voices into the design of services to make them more responsive, empowering, and relevant.

How It Was Built

  • 2016–2019: Early pilot phase and listening sessions
  • 2020–2023: Young Adult Panel formed; co-design intensifies
  • 2023–2025: Launch of RCT, digital tools, and this site
