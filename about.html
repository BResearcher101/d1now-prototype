D1 Now • Youth-Centred Diabetes Research

Transforming Type 1 Diabetes Care

D1 Now is a youth-led research programme in Ireland developing practical ways to support young adults living with Type 1 Diabetes through everyday life and routine diabetes care.

What is D1 Now?

D1 Now is a multi-year programme focused on improving support for young adults aged 18–25 living with Type 1 Diabetes in Ireland. We bring together young adults, clinicians, and researchers to redesign how diabetes care feels and works during young adulthood.

Why It Matters

Young adulthood is a time of huge change — moving out, college or work, new routines, new independence. Diabetes doesn’t pause during these transitions. D1 Now focuses on supporting young adults during this important stage.

Life changes, care needs change

Care needs evolve as young adults navigate busy, shifting lives. We explore how care can adapt.

Lived experience matters

Youth voice is central to the programme, shaping language, priorities, and resources.

Better support for the future

Insights from D1 Now aim to inform long-term improvements to diabetes services across Ireland.

Our Approach

We combine research, co-production, and clinical insight to design tools and ideas that fit real life.

Youth-centred

Young adults help guide direction, language, and the development of tools and supports.

Evidence-informed

Our work is shaped by behavioural science, research evidence, and clinical expertise.

Collaborative

We work with diabetes teams and partners nationally to support meaningful change.
