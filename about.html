What is D1 Now?

The name D1 Now reflects a simple idea: young adults living with type 1 diabetes should have support that feels timely, relevant and realistic.

D

The D represents Diabetes

1

The 1 represents Type 1 Diabetes and the people living with it.

Now

reflects timely support, designed around what young adults need in real life.


D1 Now focuses on improving care for young adults living with type 1 diabetes in Ireland. Since 2014, it has brought young adults, healthcare professionals and researchers together to understand what gets in the way of diabetes care, and to design more person-centred ways of working.
D1 Now has developed through several stages, from early co-design and intervention development, to a pilot study, and now further trial work. Learn more →

What D1 Now does

  • Brings together young adults, clinicians, and researchers to improve care experiences
  • Works with young adults living with type 1 diabetes as partners in research
  • Designs and tests ways to make diabetes care more relevant, realistic and person-centred
  • Supports better conversations between young adults and diabetes healthcare teams
  • Builds research evidence that can inform future diabetes care and service improvement Learn more about the Trial →

Who it’s for

  • Young adults (18–25) living with Type 1 Diabetes.
  • Diabetes clinics and healthcare professionals.
  • Researchers working in youth health, diabetes, behavioural science, implementation or co-design
  • Policy makers, service leaders and organisations working to improve diabetes care

Why it matters

Young adulthood can bring major changes, including college, work, relationships, independence, travel and new routines. Type 1 diabetes continues through all of this. D1 Now focuses on making diabetes care more responsive to young adults’ real lives, not just their clinic results.

🧭

Young adulthood is a demanding stage

Life can change quickly between 18 and 25. Diabetes care needs to recognise changing routines, independence, college, work, relationships, travel, and the day-to-day effort of living with Type 1 Diabetes

🗣️

Relationships with care teams matter

Young adults are more likely to engage with care when they feel known, listened to and supported. D1 Now focuses on improving conversations and building more consistent, useful connections between young adults and their diabetes teams

📈

Better support, long-term impact

D1 Now aim to inform service improvements that benefit young adults now and strengthen future care models

Our approach

D1 Now brings together young adult partnership, behavioural science, clinical expertise and real-world testing. The work is designed with the people who use and deliver young adult diabetes care, so that ideas are practical, respectful and possible to use in busy clinic settings.

Co-designed with YAP

Young adults living with type 1 diabetes (YAP) are partners in the work, not just participants. The YAP helps shape priorities, study materials, intervention design and how D1 Now shares its learning

Grounded in evidence

D1 Now uses research evidence, behavioural science and structured intervention development methods to understand what needs to change and how support can be designed, tested and improved

Built for real-world care

D1 Now is developed with diabetes teams and tested in young adult clinic settings. This helps us learn what is acceptable, feasible and useful for young adults and healthcare professionals

From idea to trial

D1 Now has grown through several stages, with young adults involved throughout. The project started in 2014 by asking what needs to change in young adult type 1 diabetes care, then moved into designing, refining and testing practical ways to improve support. Learn more about it in the publication section ->

Intervention development

D1 Now began by working with young adults, healthcare professionals, researchers and policy stakeholders to understand what needed to change in young adult diabetes care. This stage helped identify key priorities, including stronger relationships with diabetes teams, better clinic conversations and support that fits young adults’ lives

Pilot testing

The D1 Now intervention was tested in a pilot cluster randomised controlled trial in young adult diabetes clinics. The pilot explored whether the intervention was acceptable and feasible for young adults and healthcare teams. It found that the support worker and agenda-setting tool were viewed positively, while the interactive messaging system needed further refinement

Ongoing trial

Learning from the pilot has informed the next phase of D1 Now. The ongoing trial is evaluating a refined version of the intervention in young adult diabetes services, with the aim of building stronger evidence about whether and how D1 Now can improve care and outcomes for young adults living with Type 1 Diabetes Learn more about the ongoing trial ->

Governance & Oversight

D1 Now is delivered within a structured research governance framework. This includes ethical approval, sponsor oversight, study management processes, and independent review of participant safety and study conduct. These processes help ensure that the research is carried out responsibly, transparently, and with young adults’ safety and rights at the centre. You can find the ToRs in the OSF.

Independent Trial Steering & Data Monitoring Committee

The ITSDMC provides external oversight of the study. This group reviews study progress, participant safety, data quality, and any issues that may affect the integrity of the research.
