D1 Now • Youth-Centred Diabetes Research

A new approach to supporting young adults with Type 1 Diabetes.

D1 Now is a multi-year research programme in Ireland co-created with young adults living with Type 1 Diabetes. We work across clinical care, co-design, and real-world research to create practical ways to make diabetes care feel more supportive, connected, and relevant.

What is D1 Now?

D1 Now is a research and innovation programme focused on improving care and support for young adults aged 18–25 living with Type 1 Diabetes. The programme brings together young adults, researchers, and clinicians to develop tools, resources, and models of care that make diabetes services more person-centred.

Why It Matters

Young adulthood is a time of huge change — moving out, college or work, new routines, new independence. Diabetes doesn’t pause during these transitions. D1 Now focuses on supporting young adults during this important stage.

Life changes, care needs change

Many young adults find traditional clinic structures hard to fit into busy, shifting lives. We explore how care can work better.

Lived experience matters

Youth voice is central to the programme, shaping language, resources, and priorities across all our work.

Better support for the future

The insights from D1 Now aim to inform long-term improvements in diabetes services across Ireland and beyond.

Our Approach

We combine research, co-design, and clinical insight to create practical solutions for real-world diabetes care.

Youth-centred

Young adults with T1D guide our direction and shape our tools and resources.

Evidence-informed

Our work is grounded in behavioural science, real-world data, and clinical expertise.

Collaborative

We work closely with diabetes teams and research partners across Ireland.
