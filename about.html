Transforming Type 1 Diabetes Care with Young Adults

D1 Now is a research programme co-created with young adults in Ireland, shaping a new approach to diabetes care that values lived experience and shared decision-making.

What is D1 Now?

The D1 Now study is a youth-led research programme supporting young adults (18–25) living with Type 1 Diabetes. We’re working with 12 clinical sites across Ireland to run a Randomised Controlled Trial (RCT) involving 348 participants, exploring how care can be improved by integrating young adult voices and new tools.

Why it Matters

Young adults often face barriers in diabetes care. D1 Now is addressing this through tailored support, shared decision-making, and a national co-designed trial. We’re creating resources, building trust, and amplifying youth perspectives in healthcare.

How It Was Built

  • 2016–2019: Pilot phase and early co-design work
  • 2020–2023: Young Adult Panel (YAP) launched, intervention refined
  • 2023–2025: National RCT and website co-design with YAP
