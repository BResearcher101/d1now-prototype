D1 Now

Transforming Type 1 Diabetes Care with Young Adults

D1 Now is a research programme co-created with young adults in Ireland, shaping a new approach to diabetes care that values lived experience and shared decision-making.

What is D1 Now?

D1 Now supports young adults (18–25) living with Type 1 Diabetes. Through a co-designed national trial across 12 clinical sites in Ireland, we’re testing tools and approaches that improve real-world care.
